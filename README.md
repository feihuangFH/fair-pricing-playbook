# The Fair Pricing Playbook

A practical framework for responsible AI in algorithmic pricing, with applications to insurance and financial services.

**Live site:** [fair.feihuang.org](https://fair.feihuang.org)

## About

The Fair Pricing Playbook translates research from economics, statistics, actuarial science, and machine learning into a four-step workflow:

1. **Define fairness** — survey the different fairness criteria and choose one
2. **Design fair pricing** — map the chosen criterion to a concrete model design
3. **Assess impact** — trace price effects through demand and competition
4. **Audit the system** — test whether a deployed system meets the standard

Three reproducible insurance case studies provide technical depth. The four steps apply to any sector where algorithmic pricing raises fairness concerns, including credit, housing, energy, and mobility.
